# Engineering Health Review

_Weeks 1–16 (2026-06-15 to 2026-09-28, week starts) · synthetic organisation · generated by Agent 10 from validated outputs._

## Summary

Engineering health is **63.8/100**, -11.9 points since week 1 (relative index; 75 = the organisation's level in weeks 1–4). 7 validated risks, 2 critical.

_Changes compare weeks 13–16 with weeks 1–6 (medians; pooled rates for percentages), as tested._

- **Atlas is slowing down and losing quality.** Cycle time 3.2 d → 4.5 d (+39%), PR review time 10 h → 16.5 h (+65%), defects 6 → 12 per week, test coverage 72% → 63.2%.
- **Data platform releases fail far more often.** Change failure rate 5.4% → 27.1%, rollback rate 1.1% → 25.9%, incidents 1 → 5.5 per week, on-call pages 7.5 → 14.5 per week.
- **Phoenix improved.** Coverage 64.7% → 76.6%, cycle time 2.6 d → 1.8 d, change failure rate 5.4% → 0%. Its escaped-defect rate fell (6/36 vs 1/24) but is not yet significant (p = 0.14).
- **Nova's week-11 spike was a one-off** (CI failure rate 41% vs an expected 7.2%), back to normal the next week.

## How these findings were produced

Ten agents with explicit contracts. Data quality 93.8/100 (2,360 of 2,361 records usable). 291 findings were re-checked by the evidence validator: 276 passed, 11 carry a caveat, 4 were rejected. Example rejection: rising throughput at Titan correlates with longer builds (r = 0.9), but both follow headcount; controlling for it, r = -0.34.

A trend needs at least 5 weeks, a significant monotonic change (p ≤ 0.05), at least 10% (3 pp for rates) and at least 2× normal noise. All data is synthetic.
